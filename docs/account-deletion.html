2. Account-Free Architecture

SecuBuzz is strictly an account-free mobile application. Users are never required or requested to create an account, register an email address, enter passwords, or provide personal profile data to use the application.

Because SecuBuzz does not offer user accounts, our servers do not store user profiles, passwords, contact cards, or personally identifiable data.

3. How to Delete All Data Associated with SecuBuzz

Even though accounts do not exist, SecuBuzz gives you complete control to purge all local bookmarks and server notification identifiers immediately:

  1. Deleting In-App Bookmarks & Local Cache:
    • Open SecuBuzz on your mobile device.
    • Go to Settings > Data & Privacy.
    • Tap Delete All Local Data. All bookmarked articles, read states, and locally cached news briefs will be instantly and permanently deleted from device storage.
  2. Clearing Application Storage via Android Settings:
    • Open your Android device Settings.
    • Go to Apps > SecuBuzz > Storage & cache.
    • Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data. This resets the application to a pristine, freshly-installed state.
  3. Revoking & Purging Push Notification Tokens:
    • To stop receiving notifications: Go to your device Settings > Apps > SecuBuzz > Notifications and toggle notifications OFF.
    • To delete your pseudonymous Expo push token from our Cloudflare D1 database: Submit an email to support@secubuzz.com with the subject line "SecuBuzz Push Token Deletion Request". Upon receipt, your token will be permanently expunged within 30 days.

4. Data Retention Period

- Local Data: Immediately erased upon clearing app data or uninstalling SecuBuzz.
- Push Notification Tokens: Deleted immediately upon request or deactivated within 30 days of inactivity.
