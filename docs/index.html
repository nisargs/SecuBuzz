This Privacy Policy explains how SecuBuzz handles information when you use the SecuBuzz mobile application and associated services. We believe privacy is a fundamental right, especially in cybersecurity. SecuBuzz is built to deliver transparent, high-signal intelligence without surveillance, tracking, or invasive data harvesting.

1. Who We Are & Application Details

Developer: MiSo Labs

SecuBuzz is an independent cybersecurity news and threat briefing application developed and operated by MiSo Labs.

2. Information We Collect

SecuBuzz is designed so that you can browse cybersecurity news without creating an account, registering personal credentials, or surrendering identity data.

Zero Personal Data Harvesting: SecuBuzz does not collect your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, precise GPS location, photos, camera/microphone streams, financial details, or biometric credentials.

The only technical data processed is:

  • Locally Saved Articles: Articles you bookmark are stored directly on your physical device using local on-device storage. They are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you grant permission to receive news alerts, a pseudonymous Expo Push Notification token is generated for your device and stored in our Cloudflare D1 database. This token is used solely to deliver notifications when new security briefs are published and can be revoked at any time via your device's notification settings.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

SecuBuzz stores minimal application state locally on your device (such as cached feed snapshots and saved article bookmarks). This data never leaves your device and is erased if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the application.

4. News Content & External Publisher Links

SecuBuzz retrieves cybersecurity news feeds containing concise AI-assisted summaries, categories, severity ratings, publication timestamps, and source URLs.

When you tap "Read Original", you are directed to the external publisher's website (e.g., The Hacker News, BleepingComputer, CISA). Once you leave SecuBuzz, the privacy policy and terms of the respective external publisher govern your visit.

5. Hosting and Infrastructure

SecuBuzz utilizes enterprise-grade infrastructure to deliver its fast, reliable feeds:

  • Cloudflare R2: Used for static, globally distributed feed caching and content delivery via HTTPS.
  • Cloudflare Workers & D1: Used to manage pseudonymous push notification tokens securely.
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to route push notifications reliably to your Android device tray.

None of these infrastructure services receive your personal identity, browsing history, or saved bookmarks.

6. AI-Assisted Brief Generation

SecuBuzz's backend pipeline uses Groq AI models to generate factually grounded 40–60 word summaries and threat severity classifications from public RSS feeds. This process runs entirely on the backend and does not process or expose user personal data.

7. No Sale of Personal Information

SecuBuzz does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or device tokens with third parties, data brokers, or commercial marketers.

8. Analytics & Advertising Disclosures

In-App: The SecuBuzz mobile application does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Meta Audience Network), commercial ad networks, or invasive behavioral tracking SDKs. You will never see commercial popups, banners, or tracking pixels inside the mobile app.

Website (secubuzz.com): On our public informational website and privacy pages, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. This is a privacy-first, cookieless analytics service designed to measure aggregate traffic (page views, country-level geography, and device categories) without using cookies, without accessing browser local storage, and without collecting personal identifiable information (PII) or profiling individual visitors across the web.

9. Security Measures

All communications between the SecuBuzz mobile application and backend services occur strictly over modern TLS/HTTPS encryption. Administrative endpoints are guarded with cryptographically secure authentication keys.

10. SecuBuzz Account & Data Deletion Policy

Application Name: SecuBuzz
SecuBuzz is designed as an account-free application. Users are not required to create a profile, username, password, or provide an email address to use SecuBuzz. Consequently, SecuBuzz does not maintain a centralized user account database or store personal account credentials on remote servers.

If you wish to delete all data, bookmarks, and identifiers associated with SecuBuzz from your device and our systems, you can do so immediately using the following options:

  • Deleting SecuBuzz Local Data & Bookmarks: Inside the SecuBuzz app, navigate to Settings > Data & Privacy and tap Delete All Local Data. Alternatively, on your Android device go to Settings > Apps > SecuBuzz > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage. This immediately and permanently purges all saved bookmarks, local reading history, and cached feed snapshots.
  • Revoking & Deleting Push Notification Tokens: In your device Settings > Apps > SecuBuzz > Notifications, toggle notifications OFF. Once disabled, SecuBuzz stops sending push notifications to your device. To have your pseudonymous push notification token manually purged from the SecuBuzz backend (Cloudflare D1 database), submit a deletion request by emailing support@secubuzz.com with the subject line "SecuBuzz Data Deletion Request". Tokens are permanently erased within 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy

SecuBuzz is an informational cybersecurity tool intended for security practitioners, developers, and general technology audiences. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and does not knowingly collect data from children.
